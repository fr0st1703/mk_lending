- Scope: design and UX only, six landing sections, developed sequentially.
- Owner: Михаил Козлов. Visible wordmark: Mihail Kozlov, bold sans-serif. Font pairing exploration remains a separate future step.
- Approved composition baseline: hero-concept-v1.png.
